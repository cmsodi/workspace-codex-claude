# Harbor ledger

The harbor authority published its annual ledger in March. However, the ledger omits the ferry fees, which a separate office collects. The auditor found the totals consistent with the bank statements, and the board accepted the report without changes.

The statistical model is robust to outliers, so the authors kept all 212 observations. A crucial step was matching each invoice to a berth, and two clerks did it by hand. See the [full report](https://example.org/report) for the tables.
