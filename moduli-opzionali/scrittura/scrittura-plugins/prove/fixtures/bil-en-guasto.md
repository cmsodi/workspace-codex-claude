# The Event

At about 7:17 local time on June 30, 1908, a bluish light crossed the sky over central Siberia. 60 Russian settlers saw it. Leonid Kulik showed that the explosion happened in the air, because the trees at the center were still standing.

## The Investigations

The 1927 expedition found no crater, although the researchers had been looking for weeks. In England, many people read newspapers outdoors at midnight, more than 5,500 km from the site of the explosion. This confirmed what the whole scientific community had expected, and the debate that followed shaped planetary defense policy and funding for decades afterward in Washington, Moscow, and Brussels alike.
