# Tunguska

On June 30, 1908, at about 7:17 a.m., a bluish light crossed the sky. The blast flattened 20,000 square kilometers of forest, and the trees lay in a radial pattern.

Kulik, who reached the site in 1927 with Sergei Popov, saw that the explosion happened in the air. Mr. Kulik found no crater.
