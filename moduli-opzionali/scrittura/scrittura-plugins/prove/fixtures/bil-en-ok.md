# The Event

At about 7:17 local time on June 30, 1908, a bluish light crossed the sky over central Siberia. 40 Russian settlers working in the fields saw it. According to Leonid Kulik, the explosion could have happened in the air rather than on the ground, because the trees at the center were still standing.

## The Investigations

The 1927 expedition found no crater, although the researchers had been looking for weeks. Estimates of the energy are uncertain. In the United Kingdom, many people read newspapers outdoors at midnight, more than 5,500 km from the site of the explosion.
